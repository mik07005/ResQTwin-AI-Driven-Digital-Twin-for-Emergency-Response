# ResQTwin — Research Questions

## Case Study

City: Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India

Disaster: Urban Flooding

## Primary Research Question

Can uncertainty-aware AI improve the reliability and safety of
city-scale flood emergency decisions compared with deterministic
AI predictions?

## Secondary Research Question

Can a human-centered decision-support layer communicate AI
predictions, uncertainty, alternatives, and limitations more
effectively for emergency response?

## Research Direction 1 — Uncertainty-Aware Emergency Decision Support

ResQTwin will investigate how uncertainty in flood prediction
and satellite flood segmentation can be communicated and
propagated into downstream emergency-response decisions.

The system should avoid overconfident recommendations when
model confidence is insufficient.

## Research Direction 2 — Human-Centered Emergency Decision Support

ResQTwin will provide explainable, context-aware recommendations
that communicate:

- What is happening
- Why the system recommends an action
- How confident the system is
- What alternatives exist
- What limitations exist
- When human verification is required

## Optional Future Enhancement

Closed-loop real-time updating may be investigated if time and
data availability permit. It is not a primary research contribution.
